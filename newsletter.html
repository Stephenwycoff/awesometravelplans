Questions

Before you hand over your email.

What time does it arrive?

Sunday morning, usually before 8 a.m. in your local time. If a genuine mistake fare breaks midweek, we'll send a short alert — rarely, and only when it's worth it.

How do you make money?

Some booking links are affiliate links, which means we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. It never decides what makes the brief. We don't sell sponsored placements or rent our list. The full policy is on the about page.

Is my email safe?

Your name and email are stored in our customer relationship management system and used to send the newsletter. We don't sell or share them with advertisers. You can ask us to delete them at any time — see the privacy policy.

Can I reply to the emails?

Please do. Replies go to a real inbox, and they're how we find out which deals and destinations people actually care about. We read every one, even if we can't always answer the same day.

Do you have a premium tier?

No. The brief is free, and we'd rather keep it that way than put the good stuff behind a paywall. If that changes, existing subscribers will hear about it first.

How do I unsubscribe?

One click at the bottom of every email. No “are you sure?” maze, no guilt trip. You're welcome back any time.
