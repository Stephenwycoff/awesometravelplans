Legal

Privacy policy.

The short version: we collect your name and email to send you the newsletter, we don't sell it, and you can leave or ask us to delete it at any time.

Last updated: November 14, 2026

1. Who we are

AwesomeTravelPlans (“we,” “us,” “our”) publishes the website at awesometravelplans.com and the Sunday Deals Brief newsletter. For privacy questions, contact hello@awesometravelplans.com.

2. What we collect

Newsletter signup. When you join the list, we collect the first name and email address you submit. Our customer relationship management (CRM) provider may also record the date and time of submission and technical information such as your IP address for security and fraud prevention.

Messages you send us. If you email us, we receive your email address, name, and whatever you choose to include in the message.

Technical information. Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may automatically log standard request information such as IP address, browser type, and the pages requested, as described in GitHub's privacy statement. We do not currently run analytics or advertising trackers on this site.

Fonts. We load typefaces from Google Fonts. Your browser connects to Google's servers to fetch them, which means Google receives your IP address and basic request information.

3. How we use it

  • To send you the newsletter you asked for, including occasional time-sensitive deal alerts.
  • To answer your questions, corrections, and deal tips.
  • To keep the site and our systems secure, and to prevent spam or abuse.
  • To understand, in aggregate, which topics and deals readers find useful (for example, email open and click rates reported by our email provider).
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your information for automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

4. Legal bases

If you are in the UK, European Economic Area, or Switzerland, we rely on: your consent to send you marketing email (which you can withdraw at any time); our legitimate interests in operating and securing the site and responding to messages; and legal obligations where applicable.

5. Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run the newsletter and website, and only as needed:

  • Our CRM provider, which stores subscriber records and processes signup form submissions.
  • Our email delivery provider, which sends the newsletter and reports delivery, open, and click statistics.
  • Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages), which serves the website.
  • Google Fonts, which serves typefaces.
  • Authorities or other parties where required by law, or to protect our rights, safety, or property.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not share your email address with advertisers.

6. Cookies and tracking

This site does not set advertising or analytics cookies. Your browser may store technical data from third-party services such as Google Fonts and GitHub Pages under their own policies. If we add analytics later, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for consent first.

7. Affiliate links

Some links to airlines, hotels, and booking services are affiliate links. When you click one, the merchant or affiliate network may set cookies to attribute a booking. Those cookies are governed by the merchant's privacy policy, not ours. We do not receive your payment details.

8. How long we keep it

We keep newsletter records while you are subscribed and for up to 12 months afterward for suppression and legal records. Correspondence is kept for up to 24 months unless it is needed longer to resolve an issue. You can ask us to delete your data sooner.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to our use of your information, to receive a portable copy, or to withdraw consent. To exercise any of these, email hello@awesometravelplans.com from the address you subscribed with. We will respond within the time required by applicable law, usually within 30 days.

You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time using the link in every email. If that doesn't work, email us and we will remove you by hand.

If you are in the UK or EEA, you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

10. International transfers

Our service providers may process information in the United States and other countries. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses. Contact us if you would like more detail.

11. Children

The newsletter and website are intended for people aged 16 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe a child has submitted information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Security

We use reputable service providers and limit access to subscriber information to people who need it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

13. Changes

We may update this policy as the site or the law changes. The “last updated” date at the top will change, and material updates will be noted in the newsletter where appropriate.

14. Contact

Questions, requests, or complaints: hello@awesometravelplans.com. We'd rather hear from you than have you guess.
