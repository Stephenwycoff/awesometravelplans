About

We're not here to sell you a dream. Just the trip.

AwesomeTravelPlans is an independent travel publication for people who like going places but hate the noise around booking them. No fake urgency, no sponsored “hidden gems,” no 47-tab spreadsheets.

Our story

Built for people who'd rather be somewhere else.

Most travel deal emails are a race to the bottom of your attention: flashing countdowns, “was” prices that were never real, and the same affiliate link dressed up as a secret.

We started AwesomeTravelPlans because we wanted the opposite: a short, honest brief that respects your time and your budget. Three deals a week. The total cost worked out. The catch explained before you click. If nothing is good, we'd rather send nothing than invent urgency.

That's the whole promise. It's not a movement. It's just a better email.

What we believe

Four rules we don't bend.

01

A deal is a math problem

We compare against the real baseline for that route, hotel, or season — not a fictional “was” price. A discount off an inflated number is not a deal.

02

Total cost or it doesn't count

Bags, taxes, resort fees, transfers, and the airport sandwich. The only price that matters is the one you actually pay.

03

The catch gets top billing

Nonrefundable, hidden-city, 5 a.m. middle seat, minimum stay. We tell you before you book, not in the fine print after.

04

Your inbox is not inventory

One email a week. No list rentals. No “partner offers” you didn't ask for. Unsubscribe in one click, no guilt trip.

How we make money

Affiliate links, disclosed. Influence, not for sale.

Some links on this site and in the newsletter are affiliate links. If you book through one, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. That's how the brief stays free.

Here's what that does not mean:

  • We don't accept payment to include a deal, destination, or hotel in the brief.
  • We don't let a commission rate decide what gets covered. Some of the best deals we send earn us nothing.
  • We don't sell, rent, or share your email address with advertisers or partners.
  • We don't use fake countdowns or invented “was” prices to push you into a booking.

If a link is an affiliate link, we'll say so in the email or on the page. If you'd rather we didn't earn a commission, book directly with the airline, hotel, or operator — the recommendation stands either way.
