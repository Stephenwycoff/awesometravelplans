Before you email

The quick answers.

How fast do you reply?
Usually within two business days. Deal tips get priority when a fare is live; everything else gets a real reply, just sometimes a slower one.

Can I unsubscribe without emailing?
Yes — one click at the bottom of any newsletter. If that fails, email us and we'll remove you by hand the same day.

Can I get my data deleted?
Yes. Email us from the address you subscribed with and ask. We'll delete your contact record and confirm. See the privacy policy.

Do you accept guest posts or SEO pitches?
No. We don't publish sponsored posts, paid links, or “collaborations” that require us to link to a client. Please don't send them.

Do you plan custom itineraries?
Not right now. The Journal publishes detailed routes you can adapt, and the newsletter covers the booking strategy.
