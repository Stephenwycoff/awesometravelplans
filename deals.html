Our method

A deal is a math problem, not a countdown timer.

Four checks every fare and rate has to pass before it earns a spot in the brief.

Start from a baseline

We compare against the route or hotel's normal price for that month — not a made-up “was” price. A $499 fare isn't a deal if it's usually $450.

Price the whole trip

Bags, seat fees, transfers, resort fees, and the $18 airport sandwich. The total cost is the only number that matters.

Find the catch

Nonrefundable? Hidden-city? Minimum stay? A 5 a.m. middle seat? We tell you before you book, not after.
