Questions

The honest answers.

Is the newsletter really free?

Yes. The Sunday Deals Brief is free and always will be. We make money from occasional affiliate commissions when you book through a link — never from selling your email address.

How often will you email me?

Once a week, Sunday morning. Very occasionally we send a time-sensitive mistake-fare alert, and only when the deal is genuinely worth interrupting your day.

Do you use affiliate links?

Sometimes. Some booking links pay us a small commission at no extra cost to you. We disclose it in the email, and it never decides what makes the brief. The about page explains the policy in full.

Will you sell or share my email address?

No. Your details are stored in our customer relationship management system and used only to send you the newsletter. You can unsubscribe in one click, any time. See our privacy policy.

Do you only cover luxury travel?

No. We cover the full range — $39 bus routes, $180 cave suites, and the occasional bucket-list splurge. The test is always the same: is it a good use of your money?
